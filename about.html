One lot, one family,
twenty-eight winters.

Ruth Okonjo bought a closed Esso station on Maple Line in 1998 and put six cars on the forecourt. Her daughter Adaeze runs the place now. The building still has the service pit under the third bay.

Why we price this way

Haggling is a tax on
people who hate haggling.

The old model rewarded whoever was most willing to argue, which meant the same car left the lot at four different prices depending on who walked in. In 2011 we stopped. One price, posted, the same for everyone, and it already includes the safety certificate, the detail, the second key and the history report.

It cost us margin in the first year. It has since made the sales floor a place people bring their parents.

Our end of the deal

Five things we will not do.

This list is on the wall by the sales desk. If any of it happens to you here, tell Adaeze and she will make it right the same day.

01
No fees invented at signing

Admin, documentation, etching, nitrogen — none of it exists here.

02
No car sold without its history report

You get it emailed before any deposit, not handed over at delivery.

03
No repair without your approval

Every added line gets a phone call, including the small ones.

04
No pressure on the drive

You take the keys alone. There is no closing conversation in the passenger seat.

05
No car on this lot we would not lend a friend

What fails inspection goes to auction with the failures disclosed, not into the front row.
