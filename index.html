How buying here works

Four steps. Roughly
ninety minutes.

You can do the first two from your kitchen table. Nothing is signed until you have driven the car and read the numbers.

Step 01

Pick the car

Filter the lot online. Each listing carries the same spec plate you will see on the windshield.

Step 02

Book the drive

Choose an hour. We pull the car out, warm it up, and hand you the keys with no salesperson riding along.

Step 03

Settle the numbers

Trade appraised in fifteen minutes. Financing quoted from six lenders on one page, rate and term side by side.

Step 04

Take delivery

Safety certificate, two keys, full tank or full charge. Bring it back within seven days if it is not right.
