Warranty

What is covered,
in plain words.

Every used vehicle leaves here with coverage. You can extend it, but you are never required to, and we will tell you when a plan is not worth the money for your car.

Included
90 days or 5,000 km, powertrain

Engine, transmission, differentials and drive axles. No deductible, no claim forms for you to file.

Certified
12 months, comprehensive

On vehicles wearing a Certified badge: powertrain plus electrical, cooling, steering and A/C, with roadside assistance.

Extended
Up to 60 months, priced by term

Quoted per vehicle. You get the actual exclusion list to read before you decide, not a summary of it.

Manufacturer coverage transfers

Typically 8 years or 160,000 km from the original in-service date. The remaining term is on the spec plate.
