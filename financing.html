How we shop it

One application,
six lenders.

A single credit pull goes to all six. Multiple auto enquiries inside fourteen days count as one on Canadian credit files, so shopping does not cost you points.

Bank
Prime terms, 5.49%–7.99%

Best fit for established credit and vehicles four model years old or newer.

Credit union
Flexible on older vehicles

Local underwriting, and the only lender here that will finance a 2016 with high mileage.

Captive
Manufacturer programs

Subvented rates on new stock, including 0% offers when the manufacturer runs them.

Second chance
Rebuilding credit, 12.9%–19.9%

Real approvals after a consumer proposal or bankruptcy discharge. Refinance after twelve clean payments.
