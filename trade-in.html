Trading in beats selling privately more often than people expect

In Ontario you pay HST only on the difference between the new vehicle price and your trade allowance. On a $38,995 car with a $6,500 trade, that saves $845 in tax — before you count the ads, the strangers in your driveway, and the safety certificate you would have to buy yourself.
