Buying

Is the posted price really the price?

Yes. It includes the safety certificate, the full detail, a second key and the history report. On top of it you pay Ontario HST at 13% and the licensing fee the province charges, which we pass through at cost. There is no admin fee, documentation fee or etching charge.

Can I take the car to my own mechanic?

Please do. Book a two-hour test drive slot and take it wherever you like inside Waterloo Region. We will send our inspection sheet along so your mechanic can check our work rather than repeat it.

Can I buy the car entirely online?

You can do everything except the licence check. We will send the history report, the inspection sheet and a walkaround video, take a deposit by e-transfer, and deliver anywhere within 150 km. You still get seven days to change your mind once it is in your driveway.

Do you hold a car with a deposit?

We hold it for 48 hours for free, no deposit. Beyond that we ask for $500, which is fully refundable until you sign the bill of sale.

What if you do not have what I want?

Tell us the year, make, trim and budget. We buy at closed auction three days a week, inspect anything we bring in, and quote it to you before committing. If you do not like the car when it arrives, you owe us nothing.

Money

Will applying for financing hurt my credit score?

One application results in one hard enquiry. Canadian credit bureaus group auto enquiries made inside a fourteen-day window as a single event, which is why we shop all six lenders at once rather than one at a time.

I have been through a consumer proposal. Is that a no?

No. Two of our lenders work specifically with rebuilt credit, typically between 12.9% and 19.9%. After twelve payments on time we can usually refinance you into a lower rate, and we will call you when that window opens rather than waiting for you to ask.

Should I take the extended warranty?

Sometimes. It is usually worth it on a complex European car with an eight-speed automatic and air suspension, and usually a waste on a four-cylinder Corolla. Simran will tell you which one you are looking at, and you get the exclusion list to read before deciding.

Can I pay cash?

Yes, by certified cheque, bank draft or e-transfer. Canadian reporting rules mean we cannot take more than $9,999 in physical cash on a single transaction.

Trade-ins

Do I have to buy from you to sell you my car?

No. We buy cars outright and write a cheque. The offer is the same either way, so there is no hidden penalty for walking out with the money.

I still owe more than the car is worth. Now what?

We pay the lender out directly and the shortfall is rolled into the new financing. We will show you exactly what that adds to the payment, and if it is a bad idea we will say so — sometimes the right advice is to keep the car another year.

Does trading in save tax?

In Ontario, yes. You pay HST only on the difference between the vehicle price and your trade allowance. On a $38,995 vehicle with a $6,500 trade, that is $845 saved.

After you buy

How does the seven-day return work?

Bring it back inside seven days or 500 km, whichever comes first, undamaged and with the same fuel level. We refund the purchase price in full. The licensing fee is not refundable because the province keeps it.

Do I have to service it here to keep the warranty?

No. Keep receipts from any licensed shop and your coverage stands. We would rather earn the work than require it.

Do you service cars you did not sell?

Yes, any make. About four in ten cars in the shop on a given day came from somewhere else.

Something has gone wrong and I am not happy.

Ask for Adaeze by name at 519 555 0142. If we cannot sort it, you can escalate to OMVIC, the provincial regulator, and we will give you the file reference to do it.
